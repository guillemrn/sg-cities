## Platform

web

## Users

Primary visitors are prospective clients evaluating SG Cities before making contact. Confirmed audiences include urban development companies, real estate investors, educational institutions, and local governments.

Urban development companies and investors evaluate SG Cities for construction, urban planning, urban design, architectural design, and investment-risk decisions. Educational institutions evaluate mobility planning, especially school transport planning with an integrated urban view. Local governments evaluate SG Cities for technical, legal, economic, environmental, and financing feasibility support for public infrastructure.

## Product Purpose

The website presents SG Cities and explains what the company does. Its next product goal is to make the firm's project experience easier to evaluate before the visitor contacts the team.

Success means a qualified visitor first understands the company's work through projects, images, and relevant project facts, then requests information, asks for a quote, or contacts SG Cities through WhatsApp.

## Positioning

SG Cities combines real estate strategy, urban law, construction management, technical feasibility, market analysis, urban planning, mobility planning, and multidisciplinary project execution. The site should prove that breadth through portfolio evidence rather than only service descriptions.

## Operating Context

The current site is a Spanish-language Next.js website for SG Cities at `sgcities.com`. It contains a home page, service pages, legal pages, a thank-you page, sitemap, robots file, a global header/footer, WhatsApp access, and contact forms.

The site is being prepared for migration from Netlify to Vercel. The previous Netlify form flow attracted heavy spam, so future form handling should not rely on an openly discoverable static form endpoint.

The survey for the website update states that the current sections are generally acceptable and that the main change should be placing the portfolio more strategically. Visitors should be encouraged to know the projects first.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Current listed services should remain. The survey confirms there is no current service that should be removed or de-emphasized.

The site should support requests for information or quotes and fast WhatsApp contact. Future contact handling on Vercel should include server-side validation and anti-spam controls before sending leads onward.

The portfolio should support construction work, urban projects, mobility projects, and project materials such as photographs, videos, and PDFs. Planning and mobility projects should have stronger prominence because SG Cities wants to use that experience to create new opportunities with prospects.

Open decisions:
- Exact project list, project names, descriptions, images, videos, and PDFs depend on files that will be uploaded gradually to the shared Drive folder.
- Client logos and institutional references should only be published when SG Cities has permission to show them.
- Contact destination and lead-routing details for the Vercel implementation are not yet confirmed.

## Product Principles

1. Lead with proof of work. Project evidence should do more persuasion than abstract service claims.
2. Preserve the current service structure unless SG Cities changes the business offering.
3. Make planning, mobility, and construction experience highly visible to the target audiences.
4. Keep contact paths easy, but earn the ask after the visitor has seen relevant projects.
5. Treat trust as operational: accurate claims, permission-safe logos, real assets, and spam-resistant forms.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The site should remain usable on mobile and desktop. Forms and navigation should be keyboard-accessible, readable, and clear for Spanish-speaking visitors in Mexico.
