# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

static HTML, CSS and JavaScript; deployment target remains open between static hosting and a serverless host.

## Users

Developers, property owners and investors who need to evaluate an urban, real-estate, infrastructure or construction project and request the right specialist service.

## Product Purpose

SG Cities explains its work quickly, lets visitors understand four service areas, explores the portfolio in more detail, and submits a qualified project request for follow-up.

## Positioning

SG Cities brings together urban and administrative law, feasibility studies, market analysis, planning and construction management around a single project decision.

## Operating Context

The site is a public-facing lead-generation website. Submitted requests should be available to the team through a Make scenario and should not depend on Netlify Forms.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Home page must stay brief and scannable.
- Portfolio is organized into four main areas with nine sub-areas from the client's map.
- The main contact form needs a Make webhook adapter and anti-spam fields.
- Existing brand name, logo, imagery and Spanish language must remain.
- Client proof, metrics, testimonials and exact staff biography must not be invented.

## Brand Commitments

SG Cities, Spanish copy, existing logo, teal and green brand colors, and an urban / real-estate professional context.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing static site files in this repository.
- Client-provided portfolio map attached in the conversation.
- Existing service pages and imagery in `images/`.

## Product Principles

- Say enough to orient, never enough to overwhelm.
- Show the four decision areas before the nine detailed services.
- Make the next action obvious: explore the portfolio or submit a project request.
- Keep every claim traceable to client-provided information.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic headings, keyboard-visible focus, readable contrast, labels for form fields, and responsive layouts for mobile visitors.
